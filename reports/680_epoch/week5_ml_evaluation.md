# Week 5 ML evaluation — 680-epoch model

Frozen model; real observed dataset rows only. No retraining or synthetic stress tests.

## Method

182 rows, 60 ordered inputs, 12 targets; 145 training / 37 test rows reconstructed with `test_size=0.2, random_state=42`. Rows are zero-indexed below.

Inference reads the existing exported weights and scalers. Raw outputs match the original evaluation; C++ outputs clamp negative counts to zero without rounding. Errors are in vehicles over the next 30 seconds. Overall MAE/RMSE pool all row–movement values.

Volume is the sum of all movements’ historical arr30 inputs. Training-only terciles: 74, 87. Low <= first threshold; medium > first and <= second; high > second. Ties stay together. Every table row includes observation counts; empty groups have blank errors.

## Temporal overlap and chronological evaluation

All 37/37 test rows overlap a training row’s 30-second target window; 37/37 overlap its full [t−60, t+30) observation support. These are real random-split held-out rows, but not independent temporal holdouts. Reported accuracy is likely optimistic; small correlated volume subgroups are descriptive, not evidence of generalization.

Feature origins are 60–965 seconds in 5-second steps, as generated by the existing script. A new last-20% split cannot make this frozen checkpoint chronologically held out: it already trained on rows from that interval. Cleaned source data has mixed time ranges (including approximately 1.1 billion seconds); later data must undergo timestamp and recording-provenance checks before use. No chronological accuracy claim is made here. For future training, split in time and separate training/test prediction origins by at least 90 seconds to avoid shared observation support.

Feature semantics are unchanged: arrival counts sum per-five-second-bucket unique vehicle counts; speed is an average of bucket means, with the generator’s existing missing-value handling. This report does not repair or reinterpret the dataset.
